# PureFlow Solutions for Technopreneurship Project
Smart water prediction system to detect contaminants in real-time and determine if water is safe for use.

## Objectives
1. Provide AI-driven real-time water quality monitoring, ensuring households and businesses always have safe water.
2. Detect contamination early to prevent waterborne diseases with smart monitoring and predictive maintenance.
3. Provide a cost-effective, eco-friendly alternative to bottled water, reducing plastic waste and simplifying access.
